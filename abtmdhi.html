WHAT WE DO

Steel fabrication.
Project by project.

We support EPC contractors, steelwork partners and project owners with fabrication services tailored to the drawings, specifications and delivery requirements of each project.

Detailing & coordination

Shop detailing, material planning and technical coordination to turn project requirements into a workable fabrication scope.

Fabrication & machining

Built-up sections, heavy columns and beams, complex steel assemblies and mechanical platework, with machining where required.

Quality & finishing

Material traceability, dimensional inspection, weld examination and protective coatings according to project requirements.

Assembly & delivery

Trial assembly, piece marking, export packing and logistics coordination to support installation at the project site.

RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS

Corporate governance
& business ethics.

As part of Modern Group, MDHI is committed to conducting business ethically and serving the interests of customers, employees and their families. Integrity, transparency and fairness guide our business operations and professional relationships.

Code of ethics & employment policies

People & development

We invest in our people and promote a culture of continuous improvement. Our internal policies address non-discrimination, equal employment, child labor, substance abuse and employee conduct.
