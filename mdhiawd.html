FIVES GOLD SUPPLIER AWARD6 NOVEMBER 2026

Fives Gold Supplier Award 2026

MDHI was one of seven suppliers in China to receive the Fives 2026 Gold Supplier Award, presented at CIIE on 6 November 2026.

The award recognises supplier performance in quality management, delivery and collaboration—disciplines that matter throughout the execution of an industrial project. For MDHI, this recognition from Fives is a valued acknowledgement of our contribution as a supply-chain partner.

It also reinforces the priorities we bring to customer relationships: maintaining high quality standards, supporting delivery commitments and working closely with project stakeholders. We see this honour as encouragement to continue building dependable, long-term partnerships.

ALBEMARLE GOLD SUPPLIER AWARD7 MAY 2024

Albemarle Gold Supplier Award

Albemarle recognised MDHI with a Gold Supplier Award at the Kemerton Expansion Project Vendors & Suppliers Meeting in China on 7 May 2024.

Presented in the context of the Kemerton Expansion Project, the award is a valued acknowledgement of MDHI’s contribution as a supplier and our relationship with Albemarle.

For our team, client recognition gives meaning to the work behind each project package. It strengthens our commitment to responsive coordination, careful attention to project requirements and constructive collaboration with customers and their project teams—values we aim to carry into every new engagement.

EGYPT MEGAPROJECT RECOGNITIONMARCH 2017

Egypt Megaproject Recognition

MDHI supplied main structural steel and casing for HRSG packages at Beni Suef, Burullus and New Capital. The recognition acknowledges MDHI’s manufacturing and supply contribution to these power-generation packages.

The scope combined main steel and casing for HRSG installations across three project locations. This reference demonstrates MDHI’s participation in multi-site power-project supply, with the original award available for review.

Project environment
Combined-cycle power generation
MDHI contribution
Main structural steel and casing supply for HRSG packages
Recognition
Manufacturing and supply contribution across three delivery sites
ROY HILL PROJECT APPRECIATIONMARCH 2017

Roy Hill Project Appreciation

McConnell Dowell recognised MDHI’s contribution to the Roy Hill project in Western Australia. The appreciation plaque specifically records work performed on the Roy Hill Package 4 Marine scope.

MDHI’s project contribution included wharf structural steel and overland conveyor structures within the wider Roy Hill development. The recognition provides a contractor reference for MDHI’s involvement in mining and port infrastructure supply.

Project environment
Integrated mine, rail and port infrastructure
MDHI contribution
Wharf structural steel and overland conveyor scope
Location
Pilbara region, Western Australia
