**Update-
Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Company Yard

MMEC was founded in 2017 and began construction in 2019. The first phase of the facility entered into production after completion in the summer of 2020. The second and third phases are due to be completed by the end of 2021.

MMEC is betting on the benefits offered by modular construction. Significant investments flowed to build a new facility during the pandemic, knowing that selling and profiting would have to wait. However, modularization offers better quality control, faster construction, improved labor productivity, and better use of job-site space. Modular construction provides solutions for the oil & gas process and residential buildings, healthcare, mining, and a variety of other prefabricated offerings.
